algorithm clamp

given:
v - real number, where v finit, v not NaN
find:
v' transformed number in range of [0,1]

Model: sequential
terminates: yes

[Compute + Decide] C1. lower bound if v<0 set v <- 0 (v>=0) -> C2
[Compute + Decide] C2. upper bound if v>1 set v<- 1. (v in [0,1])-> done.

----
verify
termination: 2 fixed steps.
correctnes: v in [0,1] holds after C2
invariation: v>=0 at entry of C2
