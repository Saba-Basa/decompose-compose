algorithm clamp

given:
v - real number, where v finit, v not NaN
u - upper boundary
l - lower boundary
find:
v' transformed number in range of [l,u]

Model: sequential
terminates: yes

[Compute + Decide] C0. [Swap.] if l>u swap l<->u. (l ≤ u.)-> C1
[Compute + Decide] C1. lower bound if v<l set v <- l (v>=l) -> C2
[Compute + Decide] C2. upper bound if v>=u set v<- u. (v in [l,u])-> done.

----
verify
termination: 3 fixed steps, no loop
correctness: v ∈ [l,u] holds after C2
invariant: l ≤ u at entry of C1, v ≥ l at entry of C2
